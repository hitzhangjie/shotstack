# Privacy Policy

**ShotStack — Snip & Export to PDF**

*Last updated: May 18, 2026*

## Overview

ShotStack is designed with privacy as a core principle. The app captures a screen region you define, lets you arrange frames in a local session, and exports them as a PDF—all **on your Mac**. ShotStack does **not** collect, store on remote servers, transmit, or share any personal data with us or third parties.

## Data Collection

ShotStack does **not** collect any data, including but not limited to:

- Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Usage or analytics data
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Location data
- Screen captures, session contents, or exported PDFs sent to our servers (there are none)

## System Permissions

ShotStack requests only the permissions needed for its features:

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| **Screen Recording** | Capture the rectangular region you select on your display(s). Frames are processed locally; nothing is uploaded. |
| **Input Monitoring** | Detect your configured global capture hotkey (default `F`) while other apps keep keyboard focus. The same key event is passed through to the foreground app; ShotStack does not log keystrokes. |

ShotStack does **not** access your Photo Library, contacts, microphone, camera, or location.

## Local Data on Your Device

During a session, captured frames are saved as image files in a **temporary folder** on your Mac (under the system temp directory). You can delete individual frames, clear the session, or quit the app to end that session’s working set.

When you export, you choose the PDF path via the system save dialog. ShotStack writes the PDF **only to the location you select**. We do not receive copies of your captures or exports.

**Note:** Screens may show content from other apps (documents, messages, websites, etc.). You control what appears on screen and what you capture; please avoid recording sensitive information you do not intend to keep.

## Network Access

ShotStack does **not** require or use any network connection. The app functions completely offline.

## Third-Party Services

ShotStack does **not** integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

Since ShotStack does not collect any data from anyone, it is safe for users of all ages. Parents and guardians should still supervise what children capture and export from the screen.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Since ShotStack does not collect user data, future changes are expected to be minimal.
