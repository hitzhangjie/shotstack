## FAQ

**How do I get started?** On first launch, follow the on-screen guide to select a screen region. While another app has keyboard focus, press the capture hotkey (default `F`) to grab frames one at a time, then reorder them in the session and export a PDF.

**Does it need the internet?** No. Captures and PDF export run entirely on your Mac; nothing is uploaded to our servers.

**Multiple displays?** Supported. The display where the center of your selection falls is the one used for capture.
